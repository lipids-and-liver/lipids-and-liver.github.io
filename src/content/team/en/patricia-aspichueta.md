Full Professor of Physiology at the UPV/EHU, coordinator of the Lipids & Liver group and Principal Investigator at the Biobizkaia Health Research Institute. She received her PhD from the UPV/EHU (2003) with a thesis on the hepatic zonation of lipogenesis and VLDL production. Her research studies liver lipid metabolism and its role in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD), primary liver cancer and cholangiocarcinoma. She has led projects funded by MINECO, MCIU, the Carlos III Health Institute and the Basque Government.
