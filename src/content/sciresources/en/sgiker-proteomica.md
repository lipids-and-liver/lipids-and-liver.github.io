SGIker Proteomics Unit, running since 2004, offering mass-spectrometry-based proteomics (LC-MS/MS) to UPV/EHU research groups and the wider scientific community.

- Wet lab for sample preparation, LC-MS/MS analysis and bioinformatic data analysis.
- Applications in biochemistry and biomedicine, pharmacy, environment, food science and agriculture.
- Equipment: Orbitrap Exploris 240 and Orbitrap Exploris 480 mass spectrometers, UltiMate 3000 chromatography system, gel electrophoresis system and multimode microplate reader.
