Unit of the SGIker Genomics and Proteomics Service providing experimental support and advice for gene and genome studies to the Basque science network, companies and public and private institutions.

- Quantitative real-time PCR (qPCR) with SYBR Green chemistry or TaqMan probes (Applied Biosystems 7900HT Fast).
- Agilent microarray platform (60-mer oligonucleotides, custom designs available): gene expression, ChIP-chip and aCGH.
- RNA quality control with the Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer.
