IIS Biobizkaia research support platform that designs and applies proteomics strategies to answer biomedical questions. It covers the whole experiment: sample preparation, mass spectrometry and data analysis.

- Protein identification and quantification in cell lines, frozen tissue and paraffin sections; label-free, isobaric (TMTpro 18plex) and metabolic (SILAC) quantification.
- DDA, DIA and targeted proteomics (PRM); post-translational modifications; protein–protein interactions; biomarkers in biofluids and extracellular vesicles.
- Equipment: two Orbitrap Exploris 480 mass spectrometers and two Vanquish Neo nano-UHPLC systems (Thermo Fisher).
