Lipidomics and metabolomics unit of the Central Analysis Service of Bizkaia, part of the UPV/EHU General Research Services (SGIker). The **Lipids & Liver** group played a key role in its creation and development.

- Targeted and untargeted lipid analysis by liquid chromatography coupled to mass spectrometry.
- Tissue lipid profiling and lipid distribution imaging by MALDI (MALDI-LTQ-Orbitrap with MALDI-Imaging option).
- Open to UPV/EHU groups, other research centres and companies.

*Sources: [SGIker website](https://www.ehu.eus/es/web/sgiker/bizkaiko-analisirako-zerbitzu-zentrala) and the group's UPV/EHU website (accessed October 2026).*
