SGIker Analytical and High-Resolution Microscopy in Biomedicine Service, running since 1997. It provides equipment, technical assistance and specialised training to analyse the microscopic structure of biological samples, open to the UPV/EHU and to other public and private centres.

- Optical and digital microscopy: confocal, fluorescence, live-cell imaging, slide scanner, brightfield and DIC, and image analysis.
- Transmission (TEM) and scanning (SEM) electron microscopy, with biological sample processing (resin embedding, ultramicrotomy and metal coating).
- Flow cytometry.
