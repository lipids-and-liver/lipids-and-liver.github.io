Central unit of the SGIker animal facility service, in the refurbished building annexed to the Faculty of Medicine in Leioa. It is registered as a user establishment for experimental animals (EU-02.BI).

- Supply of laboratory animals for research and teaching to the university community, other research centres and companies.
- Rooms for breeding and housing rats and mice under SPF conditions, experimentation and quarantine rooms, and a room for transgenic mice.
- Technical and legal advice and monitoring of compliance with animal protection regulations.
- Experimentation requires prior approval from the Ethics Committee.
