Unit of the SGIker General Genomics Service that provides DNA sequencing and genotyping equipment and expertise to the UPV/EHU, the Basque science network, biotechnology companies and public and private institutions, for basic and applied research as well as diagnostics.

- DNA extraction, quantification and quality control from a wide range of sample types.
- Sanger sequencing by capillary electrophoresis and Illumina next-generation sequencing (NGS): library preparation for genomes, exomes, targeted panels, amplicons and metagenomics.
- Genotyping of microsatellites, SNPs and CNVs by capillary electrophoresis, qPCR or nanofluidics (Fluidigm); detection of genetically modified animal mutants.
- Equipment: Illumina MiSeq, NextSeq 550 and NovaSeq 6000 sequencers, SeqStudio genetic analyser and BioMark HD system.
