SGIker General Scientific Computing Service (IT Applied to Research), providing intensive computing capacity to UPV/EHU researchers, other public institutions and companies.

- Access to the ARINA high-performance computing cluster: more than 9,000 cores, over 440 TFlops, parallel file systems and a broad scientific software stack.
- Advice on computing strategies and annual training in HPC environments.
- Common uses include bioinformatics, molecular simulation, database processing and parallel computing.

*Source: [SGIker website](https://www.ehu.eus/es/web/sgiker/datuak_kalkulu_zientifikoa) (accessed November 2026).*
