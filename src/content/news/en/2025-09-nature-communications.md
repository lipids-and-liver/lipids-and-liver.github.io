Led by Dr. Patricia Aspichueta and published in *Nature Communications*, this work demonstrates that intratumoral sphingomyelin shielding impairs CD8+ cytotoxic T cell infiltration. Pharmacological inhibition of ceramide synthesis effectively sensitizes refractory hepatocellular tumors to anti-PD-1 checkpoint inhibition.
