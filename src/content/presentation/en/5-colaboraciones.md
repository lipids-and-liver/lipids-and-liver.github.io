The **Lipids & Liver** research group maintains an extensive network of strategic alliances with university hospitals, biomedical research institutes, and leading international consortia:

- **CIBERehd (Carlos III Health Institute):** Active participation in translational programs targeting metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASLD/MASH) and liver cirrhosis.
- **Cruces University Hospital (Barakaldo, Bizkaia):** Close clinical partnership with the Departments of Gastroenterology and General Surgery / Liver Transplantation, accessing human clinical cohorts through the Basque Biobank.
- **CIC bioGUNE (Bizkaia Technology Park):** Longstanding collaboration in high-resolution metabolomics, structural biology, and nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR).
- **Karolinska Institutet (Stockholm, Sweden):** Joint exchange programs for doctoral researchers and advanced lipidomic profiling of preclinical liver models.
- **INSERM U1016 - Institut Cochin (Paris, France):** European collaborative initiatives investigating lipid signaling, E2F transcription factor axes, and hepatocellular carcinoma progression.
- **EASL & AEEH:** Ongoing participation by our senior investigators in expert consensus panels, clinical guidelines, and international congresses.
