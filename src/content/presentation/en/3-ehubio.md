The **Lipids & Liver** research group is an active member of **EHU-iBio** (*Basque Research Centre for Integrative Environmental and Human Health*), the multidisciplinary research centre of the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU) located in the **María Goyri Building** within the UPV/EHU Scientific Park (Leioa Campus).

### The "One Health" Paradigm
EHU-iBio was established to respond to pressing 21st-century biomedical and ecological challenges under the international **One Health** paradigm, recognizing the profound interconnections among human health, animal welfare, and ecosystem integrity. The centre unites more than 100 researchers across 13 consolidated research groups from both the **Faculty of Medicine and Nursing** and the **Faculty of Science and Technology**.

### Lipids & Liver Strategic Contributions to EHU-iBio
Within this cross-disciplinary research ecosystem, our team spearheads and collaborates on key scientific fronts:

* **Exposome and Metabolic Reprogramming:** Investigating how environmental contaminants, lipophilic toxins, and endocrine disruptors alter hepatic lipid homeostasis, accelerating the onset and progression of metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) and liver cancer.
* **Advanced Lipidomics and Molecular Signatures:** Applying state-of-the-art analytical platforms (high-resolution UHPLC-MS/MS and spatial MALDI-MSI mass spectrometry) to identify diagnostic and prognostic biomarkers at the crossroads of environmental exposure and human disease.
* **Preclinical Models and Translational Discovery:** Developing 3D primary cell cultures and sophisticated preclinical models to reveal metabolic vulnerabilities and targetable cellular mechanisms under environmental and metabolic stress.
