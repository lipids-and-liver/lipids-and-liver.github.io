The progression from simple steatosis to non-alcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH) constitutes one of the most critical challenges in contemporary hepatology. Our group investigates the molecular pathways connecting cellular lipid overload with organelle dysfunction, lipotoxicity, and chronic inflammation.
