<p>Tumour development in the liver involves profound changes in the biosynthetic pathways of fatty acids and phospholipids. The MAFLD and obesity epidemic has increased the incidence of hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) and cholangiocarcinoma (iCCA).</p>
        <h4>Research Areas:</h4>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Desensitisation and Reprogramming:</strong> Identification of metabolic vulnerabilities in triglyceride synthesis and de novo lipogenesis.</li>
          <li><strong>Therapeutic Combinations:</strong> Enzyme modulators combined with sorafenib/atezolizumab to overcome drug resistance.</li>
        </ul>
