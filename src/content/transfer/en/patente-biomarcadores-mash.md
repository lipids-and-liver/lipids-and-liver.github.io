A targeted LC-MS/MS panel measuring circulating sphingolipids and diacylglycerol ratios capable of predicting active steatohepatitis and clinically significant fibrosis (F >= 2) with AUC > 0.92, providing a reliable alternative to invasive liver biopsy.
