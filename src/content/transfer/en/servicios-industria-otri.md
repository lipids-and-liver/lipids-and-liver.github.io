We offer specialized analytical, pharmacological, and pre-clinical services to pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and nutraceutical companies through three dedicated modalities:
1. **Preclinical Screening:** Cytotoxicity and metabolic efficacy assays in human primary hepatocytes and 3D patient-derived liver organoids.
2. **Comprehensive Lipidomic Profiling:** Absolute quantification of >400 lipid species by high-resolution LC-MS/MS in biofluids, biopsies, and culture media.
3. **Biomarker Clinical Validation:** Multi-center cross-validation studies utilizing human specimen cohorts via the Basque Biobank.
