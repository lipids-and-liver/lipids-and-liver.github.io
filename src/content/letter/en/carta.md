I am delighted to warmly welcome you to the official web portal of the **Lipids & Liver** Consolidated Research Group (IT1560-22). Based in the Department of Physiology at the Faculty of Medicine and Nursing of the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU) and affiliated with the Biocruces Bizkaia Health Research Institute, our group combines frontier biomedical research with higher education and an enduring commitment to transferring scientific innovation to healthcare and society.

Our scientific mission is focused on unraveling the molecular mechanisms and pathophysiological determinants of hepatic lipid metabolism, with particular dedication to metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD/MASH), fibrotic progression, cirrhosis, and primary liver malignancies (hepatocellular carcinoma and cholangiocarcinoma). Harnessing mass spectrometry-based lipidomics via the SGIker Central Unit, advanced cell and molecular biology, and spatial omics technologies, we strive every day to discover novel therapeutic targets and non-invasive biomarkers of direct clinical utility.

Scientific excellence is an intrinsically collaborative endeavor. Continuously accredited by the Basque Government as a Type A Consolidated Group since 2007, we maintain a core dedication to mentoring emerging predoctoral and postdoctoral researchers, as well as fostering multidisciplinary partnerships with university hospitals and leading research consortia worldwide. I invite you to explore our research lines, funded projects, scientific publications, and state-of-the-art facilities, and to join us on this path of scientific discovery and public service.
