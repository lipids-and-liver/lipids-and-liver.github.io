A multicenter European consortium mapping at single-cell resolution the remodeling of the lipid tumor microenvironment in hepatocellular carcinoma resistant to immune checkpoint inhibitors (anti-PD1/PD-L1). The UPV/EHU node leads the spatial lipidomic imaging (MALDI-MSI) and clinical validation in 3D patient-derived organoids.
