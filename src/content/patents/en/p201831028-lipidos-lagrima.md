### Description and Clinical Applicability

This patent originates from an interdisciplinary research collaboration between members of the **Lipids & Liver Research Group** (Dr. Begoña Ochoa and Dr. Yuri Rueda) and the Ophthalmic-Cell Biology Group at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU).

#### Key Aspects of the Innovation:
1. **Efficient, non-destructive extraction procedure:** Optimized use of isopropanol as the primary extraction solvent to isolate lipid fractions from low-volume tear film samples.
2. **High-resolution lipidomic profiling:** Enables accurate mass-spectrometry characterization and quantification of complex lipid species within the tear film.
3. **Biomarkers and ocular diagnostics:** Leverages altered tear lipid signatures for early diagnosis and monitoring of ocular surface diseases (dry eye disease, Meibomian gland dysfunction, glaucoma).
4. **Standardized diagnostic kit:** Reagents formulation and methodological design for packaging into clinical laboratory diagnostic kits.
